Voting Registration

The first step to vote is to register.

Making sure that your registered to vote is the first and crucial step towards a better change. Whether you go in person, mail in, or do it online, it is very easy to do. We provide links below to help you along the way for any method you prefer.

Deadline for ONLINE/MAIL-IN registration: October 19th, 2026. (for California Residents) *Check with your state to confirm your deadline*

Dealine for IN-PERSON registration: October 19th, 2026 OR 'conditionally' register after the deadline and same-day registration. (for California Residents) *Check with your state to confirm your deadline*

Your vote matters.
